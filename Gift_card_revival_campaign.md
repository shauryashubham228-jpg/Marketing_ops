# Project Objective

Design and execute a data-driven “Gift Card Revival + 25% Off Storewide” campaign focused on:
- reactivating dormant gift card balances
- increasing Average Order Value (AOV)
- improving customer retention
- increasing direct website conversions
- synchronizing inventory, marketing, and operational teams
- validating campaign effectiveness through structured A/B testing

The campaign combined:
- operational inventory planning
- personalized lifecycle marketing
- omnichannel customer communication
- Meta Ads strategy
- statistical experimentation

---

# Core Business Problem

Unused gift cards created multiple business challenges:

- deferred revenue liability on the balance sheet
- inactive high-value customers
- underutilized customer lifetime value
- inefficient promotional targeting
- lack of personalization in campaigns

Additionally:
- standard sale campaigns lacked segmentation
- ad spend was not fully aligned with customer value
- dormant gift card holders were not actively re-engaged

---

# Business Goals

## Primary Objectives
- increase gift card redemption rate
- improve Average Order Value (AOV)
- increase repeat purchase behavior
- reactivate dormant customers
- improve conversion efficiency during sale periods

---

# Core KPIs Tracked

| KPI | Formula | Business Goal |
|---|---|---|
| Gift Card Redemption Rate | Redeemed Gift Cards ÷ Total Targeted Gift Cards | 15–20% |
| Average Order Value (AOV) | Revenue ÷ Total Orders | Increase AOV |
| Incremental Revenue Lift | Additional cash revenue beyond GC value | Positive uplift |
| Conversion Rate | Orders ÷ Sessions | Higher campaign efficiency |
| Campaign ROI | Revenue ÷ Campaign Cost | Improve profitability |

# A/B Testing Framework

# Objective

Validate whether:
```text

```

---

# Hypothesis

```text

because customers perceive unused balances as “free money.”
```
